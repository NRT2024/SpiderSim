## Combined application of Satellite Internet, Internet of vehicles and driverless Technology

### 1. Domain Background

With the ongoing advancement of satellite internet technology, traditional flat communication networks are evolving into three-dimensional, globalized networks, providing users with broader, faster, and more reliable connectivity. In the field of smart transportation, the deep integration of satellite internet with vehicular networks (V2X) and autonomous driving technologies has attracted significant attention. By connecting vehicles, roads, and traffic infrastructure via satellite internet, seamless interconnectivity is achieved, greatly expanding the application prospects of intelligent transportation systems. In the future, satellite internet will bring more innovative scenarios for vehicular networks and autonomous driving, facilitating the intelligent upgrade of traffic management and travel solutions.

### 2. Problem Analysis

Vehicular networks and autonomous driving demand extremely high reliability, real-time performance, and low latency from communication networks. However, in areas such as highways, remote regions, or open environments where terrestrial networks are hard to reach, issues like communication interruption and signal attenuation are common. While satellite internet offers wide-area coverage and can effectively supplement these scenarios, technical challenges still exist in large-scale vehicle access, real-time data transmission, low latency, and ensuring network security. How to efficiently, securely, and with low latency transmit and process massive volumes of traffic data is the core challenge in this technological integration.

### 3. Scene Design

This scenario envisions deploying satellite communication terminals and vehicular network devices along highways, urban roads, and remote areas to provide real-time communication support for autonomous vehicles. Satellite internet not only ensures connectivity for vehicles in network blind spots, but also supports high-speed data transfer, enabling real-time decision-making and coordinated control for autonomous driving. For instance, during autonomous fleet operation, satellite internet can facilitate efficient information exchange between vehicles and with roadside infrastructure, improving fleet coordination and driving safety.

### 4. Target Design

The goal of this scenario is to enhance the intelligence of traffic systems, optimize management efficiency, and ensure road safety. Through the deep integration of satellite internet with vehicular networks and autonomous driving technologies, vehicles can access real-time road conditions, traffic incidents, and navigation instructions, easing congestion and increasing throughput. Meanwhile, autonomous driving systems can achieve more precise positioning and decision-making based on satellite internet, greatly advancing the adoption and application of self-driving technology.

### 5. Element Design

The system consists of five key elements: satellite communication systems, vehicular network terminals, autonomous driving technology modules, data transmission platforms, and security layers. The satellite communication system provides stable signal transmission for global traffic infrastructure and vehicles; vehicular network terminals enable information exchange; autonomous driving modules are responsible for intelligent decision-making and control; the data transmission platform ensures fast, low-latency data flow; and the security layer protects information security and privacy across the system, guarding against cyberattacks and data breaches.

### 6. Solution Design

The solution adopts a multi-layered communication architecture that combines satellite, terrestrial, and new-generation technologies (such as 5G/6G), utilizing intelligent scheduling and link-switching mechanisms to dynamically select the optimal communication path based on the scenario. The platform integrates edge computing and big data analytics to process large-scale vehicle data in real time, improving response speed and security. The system can also automatically adjust resource allocation according to changing traffic environments, ensuring efficient collaboration and reliable communication for autonomous vehicles.

### 7. Scene Implementation

In the pilot phase, highways, urban roads, and remote regions will be selected as demonstration sites for deploying satellite communication terminals, vehicular network devices, and autonomous vehicles, allowing for system integration testing and field demonstrations. Through continuous testing and data analysis, system parameters and coordination mechanisms will be optimized, with gradual expansion of coverage and supported vehicle types. Ultimately, a replicable and scalable technical solution and business model will be formed to promote the large-scale application of smart transportation and autonomous driving technologies.
